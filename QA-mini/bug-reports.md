# Bug Reports

---

## BUG-001: Add to Cart Delay

- Description: Delay occurs when adding a product to the cart
- Steps to Reproduce:
  1. Open product page
  2. Click "Add to Cart"
- Expected Result: Product is added immediately to the cart
- Actual Result: Noticeable delay before the product appears in the cart
- Severity: Low
- Priority: Low
- Status: Open

## BUG-002: Missing Validation on Checkout

- Description: Checkout allows submission with empty required fields
- Expected Result: User should be required to fill all fields
- Actual Result: Form is submitted without validation
- Severity: Medium
- Priority: Medium
